About us

Sumangalam Multi State Credit Co-operative Society Limited is owned by its members. The office keeps each member’s account for shares, savings, deposits, and loans. Anyone who wants to join can visit the society office with their details and the nominee details. Existing members can deposit, withdraw, or ask about a loan at the counter.

Services

These are open to members. New visitors can ask at the office about joining.

  • Membership

    Join the society and receive your own account number. Bring your personal details and nominee name when you enrol.

  • Shares

    Members must hold the minimum share of the society at the time of joining, as per the norms of the society, and may add later as per need. A dividend is paid when the society declares one.

  • Amanat Deposit

    Keep a savings balance with the society and draw from it when you need to. Interest is added as decided by the society.

  • Recurring deposit

    Save a fixed amount every month for an agreed term. At the end, the society pays the deposit back with interest.

  • Fixed deposit

    Place a lump sum for a set period. You may renew it or collect the maturity amount. The office issues a deposit certificate.

  • Loans

    Members can apply for a loan. After it is sanctioned, repayment is collected in instalments. Ask the office for the current limit and rate.
